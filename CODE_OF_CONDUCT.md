# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be constructive, assume good faith. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not welcome here. Maintainers may remove content and ban participants who violate this.

To report a violation, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer listed on the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially and seriously.
